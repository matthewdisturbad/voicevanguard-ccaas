⚠️ 1. The Operational Friction

  • False Infrastructure Blame: Contact center agents and supervisors frequently attribute one-way or robotic audio to Genesys Cloud, when ~85% of root causes stem from local domestic Wi-Fi jitter, CPU saturation, or bufferbloat.
  • Hefty TCO & Slow Tier-2 MTTR: IT support teams spend hours gathering manual packet dumps or troubleshooting individual agent workstations.
  • Chromium Degradation: Tab concurrency and accumulated browser cache lead to unexpected station de-registrations.

💡 2. The Solution: VoiceVanguard

  • Universal WebRTC Interceptor: Lightweight browser extension gathers native RTP packet statistics (getStats()) without bulky third-party SDKs.
  • Real-Time ITU-T G.107 MOS: Calculates an authoritative 1.00 to 4.50 score updated per second of active voice stream.
  • Safe Remote Cache Flush: Supervisors can remotely trigger cache cleanup with an automated In-Call Lock that waits for call wrap-up.
  • 100% Privacy Compliant: Zero raw audio recording. Zero eavesdropping. Strict LGPD & GDPR compliance.
