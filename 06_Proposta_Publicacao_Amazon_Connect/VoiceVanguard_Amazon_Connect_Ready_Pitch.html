⚠️ 1. The Amazon Connect Last-Mile Challenge

  • False AWS Cloud Tickets: Amazon Connect agents operating from home offices experience robotic audio or dropped calls and open severe tickets blaming AWS Cloud Media regions, when a relevant share of issues can originate in residential Wi-Fi congestion or bufferbloat (share to be measured in pilot).
  • Opaque Endpoint Diagnostics: Amazon CloudWatch metrics provide post-call summaries, but supervisors lack real-time per-second visibility into the agent's live browser health.
  • Corrupted Chromium Telephony Cache: High-volume agent shifts degrade browser process memory, causing silent CCP station de-registrations.

💡 2. The Solution: VoiceVanguard Sentinel

  • Amazon Connect Streams Interceptor: Lightweight browser agent hooks directly into Amazon Connect CCP WebRTC sessions, correlating telemetry by ContactId.
  • Real-Time ITU-T G.107 MOS Calculation: Continuously estimates perceived voice quality from 1.00 to 4.50 every 2 seconds using RTP metrics.
  • Safe In-Call Protection Remediation: Supervisors can remotely flush corrupted telephony cache with a guaranteed lock that executes strictly after call wrap-up.
  • Privacy by Design: Zero audio capture or recording. Designed to follow LGPD/GDPR principles.
